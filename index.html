MinustAbout

Tere, olen RebeccaHello, I'm Rebecca

Kujundan kodusid, mis on julged, praktilised ja ajatud. Julged, sest värv ja karakter teevad ruumist sinu oma. Praktilised, sest kodu peab igapäevaelus päriselt toimima. Ajatud, sest hea lahendus ei vaja paari aasta pärast väljavahetamist.I design homes that are bold, practical and timeless. Bold, because colour and character make a space your own. Practical, because a home has to work in everyday life. Timeless, because a good solution doesn't need replacing in a couple of years.

Lisaks sisekujundusele töötan teles rekvisiitorina ja kujundan võtteplatsi visuaali. Seal tuleb ruum luua kiiresti ja nii, et see jutustaks esimesest kaadrist peale lugu. Sama pilku ja täpsust toon kaasa igasse koju.Alongside interior design, I work in television as a props master and create the look of the set. There a space has to come together quickly and tell its story from the very first frame. I bring the same eye and precision to every home.

Ma ei alusta kataloogist. Alustan sinust: kuidas sa elad, mis sulle meeldib ja mis praegu häirib. Nii valmib kodu, mis on sinu nägu ja mida on hea iga päev kasutada.I don't start from a catalogue. I start from you: how you live, what you love and what bothers you now. That's how a home comes together that looks like you and is good to use every day.
